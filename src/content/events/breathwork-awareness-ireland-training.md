## Certification Path

The Breathwork Awareness® certification is a three-level path. Each level stands on its own and builds on the one before it — you can stop at the level that matches your intention, whether that's personal practice, one-on-one work, or leading groups.

### Level 1 — The Self-Healing Path

_Build your personal practice and learn the foundations._

**2 Modules:**

- **Experiential Module — Breathwave** — retreat, or 10 × 1–1 sessions
- **Online Module — Theoretical / Practical Training** — the 8-week live program described above

This level is complete in itself for anyone whose goal is personal integration rather than facilitation.

### Level 2 — The Facilitator Path (1–1 Work)

_Become a certified facilitator able to hold one-on-one sessions._

**2 Modules:**

#### Module 2A — Intensive Training

Develop practical skills, bodywork, and session structure.

→ **After Module 2A** you begin your 1–1 practice process, applying what you've learned in real sessions.

#### Between Module 2A and 2B

- Practicum sessions (1–1 work)
- Case studies and real client practice
- Integration of clinical criteria
- Personal breath practice
- Required readings and assignments

#### Module 2B — Certification Module

Advanced in-person training to refine your skills and complete certification.

### Level 3 — The Ceremonial Path

_Learn to work with groups and create space for collective processes to unfold._

One 6-day intensive module dedicated to group-work facilitation — holding the field, pacing collective processes, and working with the dynamics that arise when a room moves together.
